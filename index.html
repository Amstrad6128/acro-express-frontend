Acro Express – a free multiplayer acronym word game

Acro Express is a free online word game for friends and strangers. Board a Sky Train at the Grand Sky Station, get a few random letters, and turn them into the funniest, cleverest line you can. Then everyone votes, and the first passenger to reach 30 points wins the journey.

How to play

  1. You get a topic and some letters, for example B T V.
  2. Write one word for each letter, in order: "Bring The Volcano".
  3. All the entries appear without names. Vote for your favourite.
  4. Every vote is a point. The winning line gets a bonus, and so does the fastest one with a vote.
  5. The station winner picks the next topic. First to 30 points wins.

What's on board

  • Sky Trains for up to 14 passengers, with their own chat.
  • The Grand Sky Station: see who's online, chat, and start or join a train.
  • A Sky Passport for every passenger, with stats, best acros and stamps.
  • The Sky Leaderboard: champions of the week, the month and all time.
  • Colors: pick the look of your screen and the font of the big letters.
  • Music: choose your song for the writing time, from the Original theme to chiptune.
  • The Daily Express: the regulars play every day at 23:00 Cyprus time (shown in your own time).

Inspired by the classic acro games

If you loved Acrophobia and the acro rooms of the early internet, you'll feel at home: the same simple idea of turning an acronym into a funny sentence and letting the players decide. Acro Express is an independent game, free to play in your browser, on a computer or a phone.
